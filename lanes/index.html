Signal & Noise

You and your opponent commit supply in secret. Bigger commitment takes the lane. The only thing they can't counter is a pattern they can't find.

Rules

Each round, three lanes are worth 1–3 points. Both sides draw +5 supply (bank caps at 15) and commit any amount to each lane in secret.

Higher commitment takes the lane's points. Equal commitment scores for nobody. Whatever you don't spend stays banked. Both sides can see each other's bank.

First to 21 points wins. Keyboard: 1 2 3 to pick a lane, ↑ ↓ to adjust, Enter to commit.
